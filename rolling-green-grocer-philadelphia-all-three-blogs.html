Philadelphia · Community & workforce

Philadelphia, PA: A Local Workforce

Operations Blog · Rolling Green Grocer

Rolling Green Grocer is a Philadelphia-based business that brings groceries to lower-income neighborhoods by means of an electric bus. Groceries are offered at a lower cost than average grocery stores, and the buses collect plastic from customers. That plastic is used to make bags and containers that customers can use to refill their groceries on future visits.

As the business grows, employees will support plastic collection, customer refills, and bus operations. Philadelphia’s population and the mobile nature of the service create opportunities to reach customers across the city while building a local employment base.

Philadelphia at a glance

  • Population: The source blog cites an estimated 2025 population of 1,574,281.
  • Unemployment: The source cites a 5.2% unemployment rate.
  • Education: The source cites 35.4% of residents with a college degree and an 80.6% high school graduation rate for 2025.
  • Minimum wage: The source lists $7.25 per hour.
  • Typical salaries: The source estimates around $55,000 annually for bus drivers and $35,000 for cashiers.

How the local workforce supports the business

The original operations blog identifies Philadelphia’s population as a potential customer and employment base. Because the grocery store is mobile, the service can travel to different parts of the city and reach more residents. The blog also discusses labor costs as one factor in the business’s early operating plans.

Company news · Welcome

Welcome to Our New Home on the Web!

Madelyn Weaver, Marketing Director · October 10, 2026

The Rolling Green Grocer is ecstatic to introduce you to our new home on the web! As Philadelphia’s first electric grocery bus, we are on a mission to bring fresh, affordable food directly to neighborhoods across the city. By cutting out the overhead costs of a traditional storefront, we aim to offer lower prices to the communities we serve, including North Philadelphia, West Philadelphia, and Kensington.

We are a community-focused business built to make nutritious eating accessible for everyone. Our online vision is to help customers explore fresh market selections and stay updated with our route schedules and company news.

How it works

  • Shop online: Browse fresh produce and pantry staples and place a pre-order.
  • Meet the bus: Connect with the electric grocery bus when it visits your Philadelphia neighborhood.
  • Trade & save: Bring clean plastics and recyclables when picking up an order to participate in the trade-in and rewards program.

We want to reward customers for helping the environment. Through a plastic and recycling trade-in program, customers can save while helping keep Philadelphia neighborhoods clean.

We look forward to connecting with our community and making fresh, affordable groceries easier to access across Philadelphia.

This article is based on the supplied launch announcement. Online pre-orders, route tracking, recipe reservations, digital shelves, subscriptions, and promotional offers are described in that source but are not currently implemented as working features on this informational website.
